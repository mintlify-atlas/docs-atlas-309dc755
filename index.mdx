GreyServers is a BepInEx and MelonLoader mod for Gorilla Tag that redirects the game to **Solaristy OS** — an independent private server where all cosmetics are freely available, Steam authentication is bypassed, and the Mothership anti-cheat is disabled. Drop it into your plugins folder and you're in.

## What GreyServers does

<Steps>
  <Step title="Redirects to Solaristy OS">
    On startup, GreyServers swaps the PlayFab title ID to `1457F3` and injects custom Photon app credentials, connecting you to the GreyServers private infrastructure instead of the official Gorilla Tag servers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authenticates anonymously">
    Instead of Steam login, GreyServers generates a persistent random `CustomId` stored in `PlayerPrefs` and logs into PlayFab with `LoginWithCustomID` — no Steam account required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Unlocks all cosmetics">
    A Harmony prefix on `CosmeticsController.GetCosmeticsPlayFabCatalogData` adds every item in the catalog to your owned inventory automatically, including Builder Set starter pieces.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Removes restrictions">
    The Mothership anti-cheat module is disabled, Terms of Service prompts are auto-accepted, and ban/authentication failure messages are suppressed so you can play without interruption.
  </Step>
</Steps>
